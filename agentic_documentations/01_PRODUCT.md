# Product Specification

## Product

An AI-guided IELTS preparation platform for Academic and General Training learners.

## Product promise

Know what is holding your IELTS score back.
Practise exactly what you need.
Get useful feedback.
Measure whether you are actually improving.

## Core learning loop

1. Diagnose
2. Learn
3. Practise
4. Assess
5. Identify errors
6. Remediate
7. Reassess

## Skills

- Listening
- Reading
- Writing
- Speaking
- Grammar
- Vocabulary
- Pronunciation
- Exam strategy

## User modes

### Learn
Build English ability and understand concepts.

### Practice
Target specific IELTS question types and weaknesses.

### Exam
Simulate realistic IELTS conditions.

## Learner model

Track:
- target band
- current estimated band
- Academic/General
- exam date
- available study time
- skill scores
- subskill weaknesses
- repeated errors
- question-type performance
- writing criterion trends
- speaking criterion trends
- study history

## Critical rule

AI is the engine, not the product.

Do not build a ChatGPT wrapper.

## Trust

Never claim AI-generated results are official IELTS scores. Use "estimated band" or "AI-estimated performance."

## Content sources

Use official IELTS, British Council, IDP IELTS, Cambridge English, Cambridge Write & Improve, and BBC Learning English as reference/resource ecosystems. Respect copyright, licenses, terms, and linking/embedding permissions. Do not republish protected material without permission.
